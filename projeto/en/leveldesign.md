# Level Design Document

## General Information
- **Provisional Level Name:** Battle against the Water trainer
- **Position in the game:** Middle
- **Estimated time:** Variable, until one of the players wins (Estimate: 5 minutes)

## Progression and Objectives
- **Main objective:** Defeat the opponent and pass the level
- **Why the player is here:** To earn coins and evolve characters
- **What changed upon completion:** Unlocked the next level and earned rewards

## Gameplay Elements
- **Gameplay and Structure:** Attack, use item, and switch character
- **Important Events:** 
  - Environment switch
  - Character switch
  - Attack and Defenses
  - Item usage
  - Defeating a character
  - Special Attack
- **Novelty:** New opponent of the Water element
- **Optional Content or Secret:** None
